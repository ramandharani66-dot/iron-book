Privacy Policy

IronBook helps gym owners manage members, memberships, check-ins, renewals, and payment records.

Information stored

Depending on the features used, IronBook may store member names, phone numbers, membership plan details, start and end dates, check-in records, and payment records.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in the gym owner’s own Firebase project. IronBook does not sell member data or share it for advertising.

Who can access data

Access is limited to authorised users of the gym owner’s IronBook account. Gym owners are responsible for managing user access and keeping their login details secure.

Payments

Payment records entered in IronBook are records of payments received. IronBook does not process or store bank card details, UPI PINs, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Data deletion

A gym owner may delete member or payment records from within IronBook. For account deletion or help with data removal, contact the support details shown on the About page.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of IronBook after an update means you accept the updated policy.
